International Advisory Board

The EMERGENCE project is supported by an distinguished international advisory board of leading scholars in Old English, nineteenth-century studies, and book history.

Opportunities for Students

We welcome motivated students to get involved with the project through:

  • Student assistantships (BA/MA level)
  • Participation in hackathons and digital humanities workshops
  • Writing theses related to the project
  • Research internships

Calls for Collaboration

We warmly welcome contributions from scholars worldwide who work on:

  • 19th-century reception of Old English literature
  • Non-Anglophone medievalism
  • History of Anglo-Saxon studies
  • Archival materials related to early medieval English

If you have relevant archival materials, want to contribute a scholar biography, or are interested in collaboration, please get in touch.
