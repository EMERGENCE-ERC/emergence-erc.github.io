EMERGENCE

Early Medieval English in Nineteenth-Century Europe


Exploring the transnational reception of Old English (1786-1914)
Funded by the European Research Council (ERC), hosted by the Leiden University Centre for the Arts in Society (LUCAS)

In the 19th century, Old English poems were claimed as cultural heritage by various non-Anglophone nations, including Scandinavians, Germans and Dutch. These competing nationalistic, cultural appropriations happened against the backdrop of a growing interest in early medieval English language and literature across the European continent. The EMERGENCE project explores this transnational reception of Old English and offers a new perspective on the 19th-century foundations of Old English Studies.

On this website you can find information about the project, the project team, blog posts, past and future events, publications, forthcoming database and ways of getting involved with the project.
