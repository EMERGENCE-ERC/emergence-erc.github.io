Old English Abroad

A hybrid/online lecture series on the reception and study of early medieval English language and literature outside the Anglophone world.

The Old English Abroad lecture series is co-organized by the EMERGENCE project (ERC Horizon Europe; Leiden University) and OE-CONNECT (Una Europa; Jagiellonian University Kraków – Leiden University – University of Zurich).

We are proud to present the following speaker:

Professor Santiago Disalvo (Universidad Nacional de la Plata)
'Awareness and references to early medieval English poetry in Spanish-speaking intellectual circles (late 19th and early 20th centuries)'
21 July, 2026; 14:00 CEST
Location: Lipsius, room 2.27 (Leiden University) and ONLINE

Subsequent to the lecture, we will organize a student workshop for the EMERGENCE project, aimed at transcribing the letters of Old English lexicographer Joseph Bosworth to the Frisian scholar Joast Hiddes Halbertsma.
